MEMENTOMORI

A voice behind the victorious ruler: not a servant telling you what to do next, but a reminder of what success and power can make you forget.

The idea

A pause in ordinary life.

To periodically interrupt ordinary life with reminders of mortality, impermanence, humility, human nature, and the realities people tend to forget.

Power has a cost

Roman rulers, conquest, ambition, and glory offer a lens for examining what power can do to people—and what even the most powerful cannot control.

Perspective, not productivity

MEMENTO is not a task manager, habit tracker, or conventional reminder. It offers ideas worth remembering, not goals to keep checking off.

The attendant

Not here to flatter.

The project favors philosophy over shallow motivation, accuracy over drama, and restraint over attention for its own sake. Its voice can be direct without becoming abusive or artificially grand.

What guides it

A few things worth keeping in view.

  1. The notification is the product

    The value is in what arrives, not in how long someone stays in the app.

  2. History matters

    Roman history and the rise and fall of power are central lenses, not decoration.

  3. Accuracy over drama

    Historical claims and quotations should be verifiable and clearly attributed.

  4. Privacy by default

    Personal context should remain local unless someone explicitly chooses otherwise.
